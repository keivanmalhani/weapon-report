Weapon Report

light.gg tells you what is good. DIM tells you what you own. This tells you what you actually use.

Anyone whose Destiny stats are public, which is the default, and no sign-in needed. The name is case sensitive and the four digits after the hash are part of it.

Compare two accounts

Put two Bungie Names in and see whose habits are narrower. Both curves land on the same plot.

What this will not do

  • It counts kills, not time held. A gun you carried for a hundred hours and never fired does not appear here at all.
  • Player versus enemy and player versus player kills are pooled. Bungie reports unique weapon history as one total per weapon and does not split it by mode, so neither can this.
  • It only sees guns with at least one recorded kill. The rest of the vault is invisible, which means the real concentration is worse than the number shown.
  • Accounts with Destiny privacy turned on return nothing at all, and that is the account holder's setting to change.
